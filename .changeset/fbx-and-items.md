FBX meshes and items. The pipeline converts the game's FBX files (the 1900 ground models of items, the held items it used to skip, the vehicle bodies) through the three.js FBX loader, and a `zomboid-models/item` document shows one item on its own, on the ground or in the hand, from an item catalog.

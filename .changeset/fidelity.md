Fidelity. Characters, animals, and vehicles cast the game's blob shadows (`shadow` option); held weapons show the blood the document gives them through the game's overlay masks; a vehicle's light bar flashes with the pattern of its `lightbarMode` (`animateLightbar` option) and a new description of the same vehicle applies in place; the doors, hoods, and trunks of the cars with hinged meshes open and close with the clips and rates of their scripts, which the pipeline now keeps as skins and animations in the converted model; and the `lighting` option lights the scene for a time of day (`day`, `dusk`, `night`, `studio`, or an hour with a season and a moon) from the game's climate tables.
